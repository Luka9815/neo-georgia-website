    - term: Who can apply from Georgia
    - definition:
      - paragraph: For Strands 1 and 2, eligible Georgian HEIs and organisations of HEIs can apply directly as consortium coordinator or participate as partners. Other eligible education, training, youth or labour-market bodies can join as partners, not coordinators. Strand 3 has additional national-authority requirements; compatibility with the Georgia authority exclusion must be confirmed before preparing that strand.
    - term: Georgia’s role
    - definition: Eligible Georgian HEIs may coordinate Strands 1 and 2 or partner. Strand 3 requires EACEA clarification.
