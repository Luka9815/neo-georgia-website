  - paragraph: The National Erasmus+ Office (NEO) Georgia is an information and support office for Erasmus+ in Georgia, a third country not associated with the programme. It supports the European Commission and the European Education and Culture Executive Agency (EACEA) in the programme’s international dimension.
  - heading "What we do" [level=2]
  - paragraph: We provide information and guidance on the opportunities open to Georgia, support applicants and beneficiaries, coordinate support for Higher Education Reform Experts (HEREs), contribute to project monitoring and share results. We also support dialogue and events about education and cooperation.
  - heading "Host organisation" [level=2]
  - paragraph: From January 2027, the office is hosted by ERIDA (Education, Research and International Development Association).
  - heading "What we do not do" [level=2]
  - paragraph: The NEO does not evaluate or select proposals, award grants or select scholarship recipients. Applications and funding decisions follow the procedures of the responsible National Agency, EACEA, university or programme consortium.
