The KA archive artwork reading “ევროკავშირი საქართველოსთვის” (EU for Georgia) is EU Delegation country branding, not the grant beneficiary's **Funded by the European Union** emblem. Confirm the funding wording against Article 17 and the signed grant agreement before launch. The Georgian disclaimer is the project translation supplied and proofread by Luka, not an official EACEA Georgian translation.
