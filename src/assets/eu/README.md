# Official EU funding emblems

Use the original funded-ka.png on Georgian pages and funded-en.png on English pages. The Georgian alternative is “ევროკავშირი საქართველოსთვის”; English is “Funded by the European Union”. Both artworks contain their funding statement, so the footer does not repeat it as adjacent text. The supplied Georgian project disclaimer is unchanged.

Keep the originals byte-for-byte. The existing @11ty/eleventy-img pipeline produces lossless PNG derivatives of the complete artworks at 480 and 960 px, with intrinsic dimensions and responsive srcsets. Do not crop, trim whitespace, redraw, translate, recolour or distort the artwork. Preserve its aspect ratio and white background box. The footer uses a white surface, and its emblem remains more prominent than the typographic office wordmark.

## Georgian artwork decision, 2026-10-27 (Luka)

Supersedes the former “Rejected KA country branding” section and the 2026-10-26 decision to use English on both languages. The Commission download centre linked from EACEA distributes this archive member as the Georgian Funded by the EU emblem. The EU Delegation's EU4Georgia Supplementary Visibility Guidelines (July 2026) give “ევროკავშირი საქართველოსთვის” as the Georgian funding statement. No literal Georgian Funded by emblem exists; redrawing or translating the artwork is not allowed. Luka approved using this original on all Georgian pages. See DECISIONS.md for the explicit reversal.

The grant-agreement funding wording and website obligations still need the NEO's check. This does not authorise changing the supplied, proofread Georgian disclaimer.
